Privacy policy

Who uses it. rk-calendar-helper is used only by its author, Rob Kieser, with his own Google account.

What it accesses. Google Calendar events (the calendar.events permission): it creates events in the author's calendar and reads events near the same time to avoid adding duplicates. It does not access other Google data.

Where data goes. Calendar data stays on the author's computer and in his Google account. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access. Access can be revoked at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions.
